Getting around

How IPA Ink works

Five activities, one writing pad. Everything you write is scored against a handwritten model — the same model the Watch it written animation draws.

On the pad

▶ Watch it written

A pen writes the symbol in front of you, one stroke at a time, in the order and direction a person would use. Numbered dots mark where each stroke starts. Press it again to replay.

Guide

Turns the dotted model on or off underneath your writing. In Trace it it is on by default; in the recall activities it starts hidden so you have to remember the shape.

Ruled lines

The pad is ruled like handwriting paper. Most symbols sit between the x-height line and the baseline; tall letters reach the top line and tails such as /j/ and /ŋ/ drop below.

The score

The score is about shape only. Your writing is fitted onto the model first — scaled to your size and slid into place — so writing small or a little off to one side costs you nothing. Size and position come back as advice instead.

What you left out

Because the model is stored stroke by stroke, a check can tell which strokes you actually covered. Anything missing is marked in red on the pad, and anything that stops short in amber, with the stroke named underneath.
